## status_by_route
Query:
```
parse @message /^\S+ (?<ts>\S+) "(?<method>[A-Z]+) (?<path>\S+) \S+" (?<status>\d{3}) (?<bytes>\d+)/
| filter ispresent(status)
| stats count(*) as requests by status, method, substr(path, 0, 18) as route_prefix
| sort requests desc
| limit 25
```

## error_responses_per_hour
Query:
```
parse @message /^\S+ (?<ts>\S+) "(?<method>[A-Z]+) (?<path>\S+) \S+" (?<status>\d{3}) (?<bytes>\d+)/
| filter ispresent(status)
| filter status >= 400
| stats count(*) as error_responses by bin(1h) as hour, status
| sort hour desc
| limit 30
```

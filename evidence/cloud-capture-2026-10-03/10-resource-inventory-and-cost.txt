Interpretation:
- At this footprint a 50 USD Learner Lab allowance lasts roughly 8 days of continuous running.
- The two NAT gateways (~35% of the hourly estimate) are the largest single cost driver; they exist so that
  private instances can reach ECR, SQS, Secrets Manager and SSM without public IPs. A single NAT gateway or
  interface VPC endpoints are the main cost/availability trade-off to discuss in the report.
- Ending a Learner Lab session stops EC2 instances, but NAT gateways, the ALB, Elastic IPs and (per the lab notes)
  possibly RDS continue to accrue charges. Stopping spend requires deleting stacks, not only ending the session.
- Leftovers outside the live stacks (3 unassociated EIPs, 1 manual RDS snapshot, the ROLLBACK_COMPLETE stack)
  add cost without serving the application; their removal is a separate, user-approved cleanup action.
